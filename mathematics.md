## 📱 Apps & Interactive Tools

| App | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org) | Full structured math curriculum precalc through diff eq — exercises, video, progress dashboard | Free |
| [Brilliant.org](https://brilliant.org) | Problem-first visual learning for calculus, linear algebra, and statistics — builds intuition | Paid (~$15/mo) |
| [Photomath](https://photomath.com) | Point camera at a problem, get step-by-step solutions from arithmetic through calculus | Free tier |
| [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator) | Graphing calculator — visualize any function, parametric, or transformation in real time | Free |
| [Wolfram Alpha](https://www.wolframalpha.com) | Step-by-step solver for integration, differentiation, matrix ops, and more | Free tier |
| [Symbolab](https://www.symbolab.com) | Step-by-step calculus and algebra solutions | Free tier |

> **Use Photomath to check work, not skip it.** Desmos and Wolfram Alpha are open constantly — treat them like a calculator.

## 📖 Read — references & textbooks

| Resource | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [awesome-math](https://github.com/McHorseface/awesome-math) | Curated free math resources by topic: calculus, linear algebra, diff eq, real analysis |
| [Mathematics-for-ML](https://github.com/McHorseface/Mathematics-for-ML) | Linear algebra, calculus, and probability as used in ML |
| [math](https://github.com/McHorseface/math) | Full self-taught math degree curriculum — precalc through real analysis |
| [3Blue1Brown — Essence of Linear Algebra](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDPD3MizzM2xVFitgF8hE_ab) | Watch before or alongside linear algebra — geometric intuition textbooks miss |
| [3Blue1Brown — Essence of Calculus](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDMsr9K-rj53DwVRMYO3t5Yr) | Watch before or alongside Calc I |
| *Calculus* — James Stewart | Gold-standard university textbook, Calc I–III. Buy used; any edition |
| *Linear Algebra Done Right* — Sheldon Axler | Elegant proof-based linear algebra — read alongside MIT OCW 18.06 |
| *Elementary Differential Equations* — Boyce & DiPrima | Classic diff eq text used at most universities |
