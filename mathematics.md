## 🎯 Do — Step-by-Step Courses

*Work through these in order. These are the things you actually complete.*

| Resource | What you do | When |
|---|---|---|
| [Khan Academy — Precalculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/precalculus) | Rebuild foundations: functions, trig, analytic geometry | Start here |

| [freeCodeCamp — Math & Data Science](https://www.freecodecamp.org) | Free structured courses: college algebra, precalc, data analysis | Anytime |

| [Wolfram Alpha](https://www.wolframalpha.com) | Step-by-step equation solving, integration, differentiation, matrix operations |
| [Symbolab](https://www.symbolab.com) | Step-by-step solutions for calculus and algebra |
| [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator) | Interactive graphing calculator — visualize functions, parametric equations, and transformations |
| [3Blue1Brown — Essence of Linear Algebra](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDPD3MizzM2xVFitgF8hE_ab) | Watch *before* or alongside linear algebra — builds geometric intuition textbooks miss |

| *Elementary Differential Equations* — Boyce & DiPrima | Classic diff eq text used at most universities |
