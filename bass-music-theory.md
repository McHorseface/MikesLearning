## 📖 Read — Textbooks & References

| Resource | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [study-music](https://github.com/McHorseface/study-music) | Comprehensive music theory wiki — harmony, chord function, voice leading, rhythm, ear training |
| [TalkingBass — Chord Tones](https://www.talkingbass.net/chord-tones-the-most-important-bass-lesson-youll-ever-have/) | The single most important concept for a bassist reading a chord chart |
| [musictheory.net](https://www.musictheory.net/lessons) | Quick reference for any theory concept — intervals, chords, scales, rhythm |
| *Music Theory for the Bass Player* — Ariane Cap | The gold standard bass theory book — covers harmony, scales, arpeggios, and ear training from a bassist's perspective |
| *Building Walking Bass Lines* — Ed Friedland (Hal Leonard) | The standard text for constructing bass lines over chord changes — short, practical, with audio examples |
| *The Advancing Guitarist* — Mick Goodrick | Conceptual approach to harmony and the fretboard — applies directly to bass thinking |
| *Tonal Harmony* — Kostka & Payne | The definitive college-level harmony textbook. Heavy — use it as a reference once you're past the basics |
