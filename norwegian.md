| *Ny i Norge* (textbook) | Standard classroom textbook used in Norwegian integration courses — very practical |

> **Learning sequence:** Duolingo gets you letters and basic vocab. LearnNoW gives you grammar structure. Clozemaster pushes vocabulary past what Duolingo teaches. klartale.no is real reading practice. italki is where it all comes together. Don't wait until you feel "ready" to book your first italki session — A1 is ready enough.
