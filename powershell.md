> **Getting started with PSKoans:**
> ```powershell
> Install-Module PSKoans -Scope CurrentUser
> Set-PSKoanLocation -Path ~/PSKoans
> Measure-Karma
> ```
> Run `Measure-Karma` to see failing koans. Fix them. Repeat.
