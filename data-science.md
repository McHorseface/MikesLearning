## 📖 Read — Textbooks & References

*Open these when you need to understand something deeply.*

| Resource | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [PythonDataScienceHandbook](https://github.com/McHorseface/PythonDataScienceHandbook) | The O'Reilly book, free online — NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, scikit-learn |
| [awesome-datascience](https://github.com/McHorseface/awesome-datascience) | 25k-star curated list of every major DS resource, tool, and tutorial |
| [Towards Data Science](https://towardsdatascience.com) | High-quality practitioner articles on ML, stats, Python, and career — free with Medium account |
| [StatQuest (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@statquest) | Josh Starmer's visual explanations of stats and ML concepts — the best thing on YouTube for this |
| *Practical Statistics for Data Scientists* — Peter Bruce et al. (O'Reilly) | 50+ key statistical concepts with Python and R examples — the standard DS stats reference |
| *Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow* — Aurélien Géron (O'Reilly) | The definitive practical ML book — covers the full stack from regression to deep learning |
| *An Introduction to Statistical Learning* — James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani | The classic ML theory intro — [free PDF from the authors](https://www.statlearning.com) |
