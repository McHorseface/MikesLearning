## 📱 Apps & Interactive Tools

No dedicated Irish/British history app exists, but these work well across the history track:

| App | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) | Build spaced-repetition flashcard decks as you read — the best way to actually retain what you cover | Free |
| [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org) | Audit Irish and British history courses from top universities free — search "Irish history" or "British Empire" | Free audit |
| [edX](https://www.edx.org) | Similar to Coursera — free audit access to university history courses | Free audit |
| [Readwise](https://readwise.io) | Surfaces your highlights and notes from books on a daily spaced-repetition schedule | Paid |

> **The Anki workflow:** As you read each book, make one card per key event, person, or concept. By the time you finish the reading list, you'll have a solid deck that keeps the whole arc alive in memory.

## 📖 Read — core books (in order)

| Book | What it covers | When |
|---|---|---|
| *How the Irish Saved Civilization* — Thomas Cahill | Early medieval Ireland: monasteries, culture, Ireland before colonization | Start here |
| *The Course of Irish History* — Moody & Martin (eds.) | Single-volume scholarly history from prehistory through the Troubles | After Cahill |
| *Ireland and the British Empire* — Kevin Kenny | The colonial relationship: how England colonized and shaped Ireland | After Moody |
| *The Oxford History of the British Empire, Vol. I* — Nicholas Canny | Origins of empire — Ireland's central role in early English colonization | Alongside Kenny |
| *Emigrants and Exiles* — Kerby Miller | Irish emigration to America and Irish-American identity | Last |

> **Read this as one story:** Cahill (early Ireland) → Kenny (colonization) → Kerby Miller (Atlantic crossing) reads almost like a trilogy.
