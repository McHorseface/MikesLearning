## 📖 Read — core books (in order)

| Book | What it covers | When |
|---|---|---|
| *How the Irish Saved Civilization* — Thomas Cahill | Early medieval Ireland: monasteries, culture, pre-colonization | Start here |
| *The Course of Irish History* — Moody & Martin | Single-volume scholarly history from prehistory through the Troubles | After Cahill |
| *Ireland and the British Empire* — Kevin Kenny | The colonial relationship: how England colonized Ireland | After Moody |
| *The Oxford History of the British Empire, Vol. I* — Nicholas Canny | Origins of empire — Ireland’s central role | Alongside Kenny |
| *Emigrants and Exiles* — Kerby Miller | Irish emigration to America and Irish-American identity | Last |

> **Read this as one story:** Cahill → Kenny → Kerby Miller reads almost like a trilogy.
