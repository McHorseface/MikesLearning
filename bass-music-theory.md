## 🎯 Do — step-by-step courses

| Resource | What you do | When |
|---|---|---|
| [study-music](https://github.com/McHorseface/study-music) | Work through harmony and bass chapters first | Start here |
| [musictheory.net](https://www.musictheory.net/lessons) | Complete intervals, chord construction, scale degrees | Fill gaps |
| [TalkingBass](https://www.talkingbass.net) | Chord tones, arpeggios, walking bass, playing over changes | Bass-specific |
| [Teoria](https://www.teoria.com) | Ear training drills | Ear training |
| [Zen of Bass (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@ZenOfBass) | Role of bass rhythmically and harmonically | Concepts |

> **The key insight:** A chord chart tells you which notes are *home*, *tension*, and *movement*. Start with TalkingBass “chord tones” — that’s the unlock.
