## 📖 Read — references & textbooks

| Resource | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [PythonDataScienceHandbook](https://github.com/McHorseface/PythonDataScienceHandbook) | Free O’Reilly book — NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, scikit-learn |
