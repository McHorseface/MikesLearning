## 📱 Apps & Interactive Tools

| App | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| [Yousician](https://yousician.com) | Listens to your playing via mic and gives real-time feedback — gamified bass lessons; essentially Duolingo for bass | Free tier |
| [MyMusicTheory](https://www.mymusictheory.com) | Highly-rated theory app with graded exercises from beginner through advanced; tracks progress | Free tier |
| [Tenuto](https://www.musictheory.net/tools) | Ear training from the makers of musictheory.net — intervals, chord ID, rhythm reading | Paid |
| [Teoria](https://www.teoria.com) | Ear training + theory exercises: intervals, chord identification, rhythm | Free |
| [Chordify](https://chordify.net) | Point it at any song and get a real-time chord chart — ideal for learning songs by ear | Free tier |
| [GuitarTuna](https://yousician.com/guitartuna) | Tuner + basic technique trainer | Free |
| [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) | Build flashcard decks for chord shapes, intervals, and scales | Free |

> **Start with Yousician** for technique feedback and **MyMusicTheory** for theory concepts. Use Chordify to apply theory to real songs immediately.

## 🎯 Do — step-by-step courses

| Resource | What you do | When |
|---|---|---|
| [study-music](https://github.com/McHorseface/study-music) | Work through the harmony and bass chapters first | Start here |
| [musictheory.net](https://www.musictheory.net/lessons) | Complete intervals, chord construction, and scale degrees | Fill gaps |
| [TalkingBass](https://www.talkingbass.net) | Chord tones, arpeggios, walking bass, playing over changes | Bass-specific |
| [Teoria](https://www.teoria.com) | Ear training drills — intervals, chord ID, rhythm | Ear training |
| [Zen of Bass (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@ZenOfBass) | Role of the bass rhythmically and harmonically, locking with the drummer | Concepts |

> **The key insight:** A chord chart tells you which notes are *home*, which are *tension*, and which create *movement*. Start with TalkingBass "chord tones" — that's the unlock.

## 📖 Read — textbooks & references

| Resource | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [study-music](https://github.com/McHorseface/study-music) | Comprehensive theory wiki — harmony, chord function, voice leading, rhythm, ear training |
| [TalkingBass — Chord Tones](https://www.talkingbass.net/chord-tones-the-most-important-bass-lesson-youll-ever-have/) | The single most important concept for a bassist reading a chord chart |
| *Music Theory for the Bass Player* — Ariane Cap | Gold standard bass theory book — harmony, scales, arpeggios, ear training from a bassist's perspective |
| *Building Walking Bass Lines* — Ed Friedland | Standard text for constructing bass lines over chord changes |
| *The Advancing Guitarist* — Mick Goodrick | Conceptual harmony and fretboard thinking — applies directly to bass |
| *Tonal Harmony* — Kostka & Payne | Definitive college-level harmony textbook — use as reference once past basics |
